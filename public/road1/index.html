What you already lead

Four things hold the business up

You are leading all four right now, whether you chose to or not. Most founders are strong in two and guessing at the other two.

One

Lead yourself

  • Your hours, counted — not remembered
  • What you refuse, even when it pays
  • The decision you have been carrying for months

Two

Lead your people

  • What you expect and have never said
  • The conversation you keep rehearsing
  • Who is waiting on you without telling you

Three

Lead your business

  • What breaks the week you are away
  • What one job earns after the rework
  • The part that only exists in your head

Four

Lead the market

  • Who actually pays you, by name
  • Why they said yes, in their words
  • What it costs them to carry the problem

Everything you are trying to fix sits in one of those four. Work the wrong one and nothing moves.

So the whole job is finding out which one — and fixing that one first.

And you never drop the other three. Let’s lay the twelve weeks out flat.
