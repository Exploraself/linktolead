Why good founders miss it.

You are not blind. You are standing very close. Every founder who has grown a business has had to step back far enough to see the shape of it, and it is far easier with someone beside you.

You’re inside the picture.

The closer you are to the work, the less of its shape you can see. That is not a weakness. It is simply where you are standing.

Busy looks like progress.

A full week feels like movement. The evidence — your calendar, your last ten customers, your unanswered messages — often says something else.

The hard part is human.

Saying no. Naming the cost. Having the conversation you keep rehearsing. Holding the standard on a bad week. That is where a business actually turns.

A machine can add up your week in seconds. It cannot decide what you will stop doing, or say the sentence to the person who needs to hear it. That part is yours — and it is where growth starts.
