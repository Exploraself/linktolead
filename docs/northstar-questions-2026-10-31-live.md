# The Leader’s North Star questions, as live on 31 Oct 2026

Kept word for word before the rewrite in PR #80 (Alexandru: “these are good questions”). The ids, stages and domains are the same in both versions, so any question can be brought back by copying its text and prompt into public/northstar/model.js.

## Building

**q1 · Lead yourself**  
Do you know what you want this business to give you in life?  
_Open your calendar and budget. Write one dated example from them. Think about money, free time, health and the people you care about. What must success protect?_

**q2 · Lead yourself**  
Do you know how much time and money you can afford to put at risk?  
_Open your calendar and budget. Write one dated example from them. What are you still guessing about? Decide how much you can spend finding out._

**q3 · Lead your people**  
Have you talked with the people affected about the time and money this business needs?  
_Open your recent messages or meeting notes. Name the person and the agreement or unanswered question. Think about your partner, family or anyone helping you. Do they know what to expect?_

**q4 · Lead your people**  
Do you ask someone you trust to tell you what might be wrong with your idea?  
_Open your recent messages or meeting notes. Name the person and the agreement or unanswered question. Choose someone who can be honest with you. What would they warn you about?_

**q5 · Lead your business**  
Do you know what you sell, what it costs you and how long it takes?  
_Open your last quote, bill and job notes. Copy the costs and hours you can find. Count the work, materials, fees and time needed to serve one customer—including your own time._

**q6 · Lead your business**  
Do you know how much you need to sell and keep in the bank each month?  
_Open your last quote, bill and job notes. Copy the costs and hours you can find. Check both the bills you need to cover and when customers actually pay you._

**q7 · Lead the market**  
Have you asked customers about their problem and tried selling them a solution?  
_Open your customer messages and sales records. Copy what someone asked for and whether they paid. A nice comment is encouraging. Has anyone actually agreed to pay?_

**q8 · Lead the market**  
Can you explain who your offer helps and what they will get?  
_Open your customer messages and sales records. Copy what someone asked for and whether they paid. Could a customer explain it back to you? Be clear about what is included and what is not._

## Growing

**q9 · Lead yourself**  
Do you check each week whether your time went to the right things?  
_Open last week’s calendar or time log. Write the activity and hours you actually recorded. Which tasks helped customers or brought sales? Which kept you busy without moving things forward?_

**q10 · Lead yourself**  
Can you work toward your income goal without wearing yourself out?  
_Open last week’s calendar or time log. Write the activity and hours you actually recorded. Does your plan leave room for rest, relationships and the unexpected?_

**q11 · Lead your people**  
Do customers and people helping you know what is included and what happens if things change?  
_Open the latest customer agreement or feedback message. Copy the expectation or issue you can point to. Where do unclear expectations create extra work, delays or frustration?_

**q12 · Lead your people**  
Do you ask for honest feedback and deal with difficult conversations early?  
_Open the latest customer agreement or feedback message. Copy the expectation or issue you can point to. Is there someone you need to talk with? What gets harder if you keep waiting?_

**q13 · Lead your business**  
Do you check what is left after costs, what money arrived and how many hours you worked?  
_Open this month’s sales, spending and delivery records. Copy the entry you checked. Use the same month for all three. Selling more does not always mean having more cash or time._

**q14 · Lead your business**  
Can you deliver your main product or service well without starting from scratch each time?  
_Open this month’s sales, spending and delivery records. Copy the entry you checked. Which steps do you repeat? Where do mistakes or repeated work cost you time?_

**q15 · Lead the market**  
Do you know where new customers come from and what you spend to get them?  
_Open your enquiry list, sales records and customer messages. Count the relevant entries and write what they show. For example: social media, referrals or ads. Count the spending and the customers from the same source._

**q16 · Lead the market**  
Do you know why customers come back, recommend you or stop buying?  
_Open your enquiry list, sales records and customer messages. Count the relevant entries and write what they show. Ask people who left too—not only your happiest customers._

## Scaling

**q17 · Lead yourself**  
Can you let someone else make a decision without taking it back?  
_Open your latest approval or handover messages. Name a decision that moved or waited for you. Have you explained the result you need? Different from your way does not always mean wrong._

**q18 · Lead yourself**  
Do you notice when wanting everything done your way slows the business?  
_Open your latest approval or handover messages. Name a decision that moved or waited for you. Where does work wait for you even though someone else could move it forward?_

**q19 · Lead your people**  
Does everyone know what they can decide and when they need your help?  
_Open your team’s task list and last review notes. Name an owner and the decision they could make. For five common decisions, could each person name who has the final say?_

**q20 · Lead your people**  
Can people tell you something is going wrong and learn from mistakes?  
_Open your team’s task list and last review notes. Name an owner and the decision they could make. Would someone warn you early about a missed deadline or a wrong forecast?_

**q21 · Lead your business**  
Before taking on more work, do you check what extra help will cost and how you will pay for it?  
_Open the delivery schedule, supplier quotes and payment dates. Copy the hours and costs that support your answer. Include hiring, training and the time before new sales turn into money in the bank._

**q22 · Lead your business**  
Can the main work carry on for a while when you are not there?  
_Open the delivery schedule, supplier quotes and payment dates. Copy the hours and costs that support your answer. Has someone else actually used your process? Notice what still stops without you._

**q23 · Lead the market**  
Have you tested whether a new customer group or sales channel will still leave money after costs?  
_Open sales by customer or channel. Copy the actual sales or test result you checked. What worked for your current customers may need changing for the next group._

**q24 · Lead the market**  
Do you know what would happen if your biggest customer or main sales channel disappeared?  
_Open sales by customer or channel. Copy the actual sales or test result you checked. How much of your income depends on one customer, website, platform or relationship?_

## Leading

**q25 · Lead yourself**  
Do you know what enough looks like—and why you want the next goal?  
_Open your calendar, commitments and budget. Name the entry you kept, changed or stopped. Is this goal yours? Would you still want it if nobody else knew about it?_

**q26 · Lead yourself**  
Do you make time to question your plans and stop work that no longer matters?  
_Open your calendar, commitments and budget. Name the entry you kept, changed or stopped. Is anything continuing mainly because you have already put a lot into it?_

**q27 · Lead your people**  
Are you helping other people learn to lead and decide without you?  
_Open role descriptions, handover notes and meeting decisions. Name who has actually taken ownership. Who could take a meaningful decision off your plate? What do they need to learn next?_

**q28 · Lead your people**  
If you were away, would people know who is responsible and who steps in?  
_Open role descriptions, handover notes and meeting decisions. Name who has actually taken ownership. Think about customer calls, payments and difficult decisions—not just daily tasks._

**q29 · Lead your business**  
Do you compare your options before committing money and time?  
_Open your spending decisions and disruption notes. Name the option compared or response tested. What else could the same money or attention do? What is the most you can afford to lose?_

**q30 · Lead your business**  
Have you tested how the business would handle a serious disruption?  
_Open your spending decisions and disruption notes. Name the option compared or response tested. What happens if payments arrive late, delivery stops or a key person is away?_

**q31 · Lead the market**  
Do you check how the business affects customers, your people and the wider world?  
_Open customer outcomes, feedback and sales history. Copy an observed result or changed customer request. Look for unwanted effects as well as good results. What does your usual dashboard miss?_

**q32 · Lead the market**  
Do you watch how customer needs are changing and adjust your plans?  
_Open customer outcomes, feedback and sales history. Copy an observed result or changed customer request. What might customers want next? Who is already noticing the change?_
