## 2. What you will have after twelve weeks

Use as a list with ticks. All eight are real parts of “My Blueprint”, built only from what the founder writes.

1. **You, as a leader.** The rule that decided your worth, tested. Your rule for decisions in a storm. Your best hours, guarded. Who you are now, in one sentence.
2. **Your people.** One task handed over, and moved up a step. One area with an owner, a limit and a “call me when”. The conversation you kept postponing, held.
3. **What you sell.** Your offer in one line. Three price levels. The price you never go below, worked out from the real hours.
4. **Your marketing.** Your clients’ fear and hope, in their own words. Your advantage, with proof. Ten people you already know, contacted. One partner asked for introductions.
5. **Your sales.** A weekly selling hour. A warm list you can see. What to say after the price, and when someone says “let me think”.
6. **Your money.** Your enough, as a number. Five numbers read every week. The money owed to you, asked for. A one-page cash view with an alert line.
7. **Your systems.** One process in ten steps someone else can follow. An operations page your team can run the week from. A weekly review of ten minutes.
8. **Your next twelve weeks.** One number with two checkpoints. One small market test. One thing you say no to, and one thing you keep for your health, your family or your rest.

## 3. How a week works (one short section)

> **Tap. Slide. Write once. Done when.**
> Each domain takes a few minutes: tap the line that sounds like you, slide to the honest number, write one sentence with a real name and a real day. Then you see how you will know on Sunday that it is done, and say how sure you are.
>
> **Every Sunday, ten minutes.** The next level opens by asking what happened: Done, Partly, Not yet. A no is information, not a verdict. Not done? You get a smaller step.
>
> **Every few weeks, a reading.** What you did, how good your estimates were, what you believed before and what you believe now.

## 5. Answers to the usual doubts

| They think | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| “I don’t have time.” | A few minutes per domain, ten minutes on Sunday. The actions are things you already need to do: an invoice, a price, a conversation. The programme gives them a day. |
| “I’ve done courses. Nothing changed.” | Courses end in insight. Every domain here ends in one action with a day, and the next week starts by asking whether it happened. |
| “I’m not a writer.” | You tap and slide first. Then a few words per blank: a name, a day, a number. |
| “I don’t have a team yet.” | Every step has a version for a founder alone: a tool, a template, or a freelancer for two hours. |
| “I don’t have clients yet.” | Ask three people who have the problem you solve. The programme says how, word for word. |
| “Is this therapy?” | No. It is a self-reflection and planning tool for founders, built on Christian values of service and integrity. It asks what to open, count and write, not how you feel about it, though it does name feelings when they decide your week. |
